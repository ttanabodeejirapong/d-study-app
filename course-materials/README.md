- The five canonical current annotated lecture PDFs are kept in the owner's private course-source archive.
- Old exams, older lecture collections, exercises, student notes, textbooks, and other historical materials remain at their original external locations and are referenced here only where intentionally public.
- Professor teaching recordings remain YouTube links only.

- `old-exams/README.md`
- `old-lectures/README.md`
